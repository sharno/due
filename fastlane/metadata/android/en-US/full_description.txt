Due is a deliberately small, offline todo list.

Every todo has a required date and time, which default to today and now. When an unfinished todo becomes due, Due keeps a persistent notification visible with the outstanding work until it is completed.

Features:

• Create, complete, and delete todos
• Required due date and time
• Persistent notifications for overdue work
• One-tap completion from the notification
• Restores reminders after reboot and time or time-zone changes
• No account, ads, analytics, tracking, or network connection

Due stores all todos only on your device.
